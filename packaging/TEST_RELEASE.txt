Black Mesa VR — drop-in (2026-09-04)
====================================

Close Black Mesa first. Copy this folder into Steam\steamapps\common\Black Mesa
so d3d9.dll lands in ALL three:

  Black Mesa\d3d9.dll
  Black Mesa\bin\d3d9.dll
  Black Mesa\bin\thirdparty\dxvk-windows-x86\d3d9.dll

Keep a tuned VR\config.txt if you already have one.

This build
----------
- VR main menu with a cursor. Aim it with a motion controller or navigate
  with buttons.
- Pause menu in VR (same 2D panel path).
- HEV gloves pick up environment lighting and metal reflections.
- Crossbow zoom is smoothed.
- TAU cannon beam effects and impacts fire from the gun, not the eyes.
- Gluon gun impact decals sit on the impact surface.
- Crowbar swings are more reliable.
- Blue Shift weapon wheel and wrist HUD use Calhoun blue.
- Hands no longer bob up and down with head movement.
- Bare hands until you pick up the HEV suit. Blue Shift stays bare-handed
  the whole campaign (Calhoun never wears HEV).
- Quest 3: Meta Link OpenXR runtime, Steam Link, or Virtual Desktop with VDXR.
  Do not pick SteamVR as Virtual Desktop's OpenXR runtime (upside-down).
